# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **A wheel on your board.** Type a name in the Draw Lots panel and a wheel appears on the board. Add up to 20 names or options, edit them in place, and the wheel updates by itself.
- **Spin to pick.** Click Spin in the middle of the wheel. It turns, slows down and lands on a random name, which is highlighted with a "Picked" label underneath.
- **Hand-drawn style.** Pastel wedges, inky outlines and a wobbly rim. Every wheel has its own wobble, and everyone on the board sees the same wheel.
- **Sound and motion.** A click for every wedge that passes the pointer, a chime when the wheel lands, and a pointer that wiggles as it ticks. Mute it from the panel; reduced-motion settings turn the wiggle off.
- **Remove picked names.** Turn it on to take each picked name off the wheel before the next spin. Picked names stay in the list with a check mark, and Reset picks brings them all back.
- **Images per item.** Add a picture to any name and it shows on the wheel (up to 10 items) and in the result.
- **Works with Thai.** Thai names and mixed Thai–English names render correctly on the wheel.
